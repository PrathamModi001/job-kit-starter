About Fynd:
Fynd is a frontier technology company. We started at the intersection of technology and retail because that is where technology was the least available. Over the years, we became one of India’s largest retail technology platforms. But retail was the entry point, not the boundary.
Today, Fynd builds intelligent software that runs business operations. Not tools that help people work faster, but systems that absorb entire functions: manufacturing, marketing, logistics, commerce, quality control. We sit inside our customers’ businesses, harvest deep domain context, and build AI systems that operate autonomously. We are expanding from retail into manufacturing, generative media, physical AI, and healthcare.

Role Overview:
Own the design, development, and operation of scalable backend services and AI-enabled capabilities that power core product experiences. This role is critical in building reliable, high-performance systems, strengthening production health through observability and incident response, and translating complex business needs into resilient engineering solutions.

What will you do at Fynd?
- Design and develop backend services, APIs, and data models using Python and modern application frameworks.
- Build and maintain scalable, secure, and high-availability systems that support product growth and operational efficiency.
- Implement asynchronous processing, background jobs, and event-driven workflows to improve throughput and responsiveness.
- Contribute to AI engineering efforts by integrating agentic AI capabilities into backend workflows and product features.
- Monitor production systems, improve observability, and support incident response, root-cause analysis, and post-incident remediation.
- Optimize service performance, reliability, and resource usage through profiling, tuning, and engineering best practices.
- Collaborate with product, platform, and engineering teams to deliver robust technical solutions from concept to production.
- Participate in deployment, troubleshooting, and operational support across containerized and cloud-native environments.
